# Cover letter shape

Guidance for drafting `cover-letter.yaml` in each application folder.

- **Length:** most of a page, not more. Five paragraphs. As rendered,
  the letter must end at least three quarters of the way down the page; the
  template hard-fails below 66%, and `render/render.sh` prints the page-fill
  percentage and warns below 75%. A warning means extend the letter before it
  goes anywhere.
- **Both directions (Sam, 2026-07-22):** the letter covers both why the
  employer would benefit from hiring the user and why the user would benefit from
  working there. The second half is the one drafts usually miss; give it its
  own paragraph rather than a clause.
- **Order:** why-them before what-you-bring. Open with a specific, genuine reason this role or company is of interest, before turning to fit.
- **Evidence:** real projects and outcomes, drawn from `core/master-cv.md` and `core/stories.md`. No fabrication, no invented metrics.
- **Voice:** the user's own, per `core/voice.md`. Not the AI's default register.
- **Close:** direct and simple, no hard sell.

Treat every draft as a starting point for the user to shape, not a finished letter.
