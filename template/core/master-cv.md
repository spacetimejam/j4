# Master CV

The synthesised, complete record of the user's career: every role, achievement and skill worth drawing on, in full, before any tailoring cuts it down for a specific application.

## How to fill this

Synthesised from the user's existing CV during onboarding (see `core/intake.md`), then extended over time as new evidence surfaces. Per-role CVs in `applications/<role-slug>/cv.yaml` are always a cut-down, re-angled version of what lives here, never the other way round.
