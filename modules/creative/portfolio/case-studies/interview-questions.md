# Interview questions

A generic question set for turning a portfolio project into raw material for a case study. One section per stage of the story.

## How to use this

Work through the sections for one project at a time. Record answers however is easiest, a voice note works well, then transcribe it or hand the transcript to the AI. Answers should be raw, not polished: full sentences aren't needed, the point is to get everything relevant onto the page. Budget 10 to 15 minutes per project. Skip questions that don't apply rather than forcing an answer.

## 1. The Setup

- Who was the client or brand, and what was the goal?
- What was the brief, as it was actually given? *Not the tidied-up version, the real one, however vague or specific it was.*
- When did this happen, and was it in-house, agency, or freelance?

## 2. The Challenge

- What made this project tricky or different from the usual run of work?
- What were the constraints? *Budget, timeline, legacy brand guidelines, a difficult stakeholder, technical limits, whatever applied.*
- What made you nervous about it?

## 3. Your Role

- What did you specifically do, as distinct from the team?
- What was the team structure? *Describe roles, not names, e.g. "one other designer, an account manager, and a client-side marketing lead."*
- Were there decisions you made that changed the direction of the work?

## 4. The Process

- How did the work develop, from the first response to the brief through to the finished piece?
- Was there a moment where something shifted, a rejected first direction, a piece of feedback that changed the approach, a constraint that turned into an opportunity?
- What tools and process did you use?

## 5. The Work

- Describe the finished piece in a sentence or two, as if to someone who has never seen it.
- Where did people encounter it, and at what scale? *A single flagship store, a national rollout, an app used by millions, a one-off event.*

## 6. The Outcome

- What happened after launch? *Numbers, feedback, awards, whatever exists.*
- If there's no hard number, what's the best honest proxy? *Scale, duration, or adoption, see the accuracy rule in `README.md`.*

## 7. The Reflection

- What are you proudest of in this project?
- What would you do differently now?
- What does this project show about you that your other work doesn't?

## Prioritising projects

Not every project on the site needs a full case study. Pick three to five that, together, tell a clear story of range, covering the different kinds of work, sectors and skills the search is targeting, rather than five variations on the same strength.
