# Prompt: synthesis

**When to use:** stage 3 of the case-study method, once the interview questions (and any tailored follow-ups) have been answered. Paste this prompt to the AI along with the raw answers and any artifacts (existing copy, briefs, images described in `portfolio/site.md`).

---

You are an expert case-study writer. The audience is recruiters and hiring managers in the user's field, people who skim quickly and are judging whether to shortlist. Write a case study of 1,000 to 1,500 words.

Before writing, review all the material provided: the raw interview answers, any existing copy, and the project's entry in `portfolio/site.md`. Read `core/voice.md` and match the user's voice, not a generic case-study register. Read `core/profile.md` for the direction of the current search, and make sure the case study foregrounds the skills and experience that search is targeting.

If any detail you need to write a clear paragraph is missing from the material, ask for it rather than inventing it. Do not fabricate metrics, quotes or sources. Numbers must come from the material provided; where there's no hard number, use an honest proxy (scale, duration, adoption) rather than a guess dressed up as fact.

Structure: open with the outcome and the challenge, so a skimming reader gets the headline in the first few lines. Then tell the story in the order the interview answers give it, setup through to reflection. Close by connecting the outcome back to what was learned, rather than just restating the result.
